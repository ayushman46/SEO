# R4: Design and UX review (template tells, hierarchy, type, collage, report UX)

Reviewer brief: judge seenhq.vercel.app as an awwwards juror would, looking for generic "AI-generated" design and weak UX.
Read-only review. No code was changed.

## How this was done, and what it cannot claim

- Evidence: the live HTML of `/`, `/audit`, `/services`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/methodology`, `/bot`, `/sample-report` (fetched with curl, all 200), plus a close read of `src/app/(marketing)/**`, `src/components/**`, `src/app/globals.css` and `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- No screenshots were available. Every pixel figure below is computed from the Tailwind classes and clamp() tokens at 360, 768, 1280 and 1920 px, not measured in a browser. Figures are rounded and marked "approx.". Treat the four items tagged **[verify]** as predictions to confirm in a browser before acting.
- `/sample-report` was used as the rendering proxy for the unlocked audit report (same `FullReport`, `ScoreSummary`, `QuotePanel`, `FixTray` components).
- Line numbers refer to the files as they were on 2026-11-23. Another agent is editing frontend code, so lines may have drifted.

## Verdict in five lines

1. The home hero collage is a real idea (an eye looking back through a hole torn in the sky). Almost nothing after it has an idea of its own: sections 2 to 11 are the same kicker, serif heading, grey paragraph, then cards or a list.
2. Blue is not "one accent". It is on segmented controls, check discs, the hamburger, numerals and up to 60 fills per page, so the primary action never wins.
3. The collage is a system on the home page and wallpaper everywhere else: the same sky strip, the same moon in the same place, the same eye card closing five pages.
4. The audit report, the actual product, is the weakest page for scannability: 18 fully expanded cards, no navigation on phones, an AI card ahead of the top fixes, and a fix tray that can cover the unlock button.
5. Type is close but undisciplined: about 14 ad-hoc serif sizes, four near-identical body sizes, measures up to 100 characters, and rupee prices that fall out of the serif.

## Three signature moments

Each is one idea, tied to the brand theme of being seen, built from what is already in the repo, with transform or opacity animation only and no new dependencies.

### 1. Home: the eye notices you

**Idea.** The painted eyes in the torn hole blink once when the visitor puts the cursor in the URL field. The site is about being seen; this is the moment the page looks back.

**Build.**
- In `HeroCollage.tsx`, inside the ellipse `TornFrame` (after the image, before the shading div at line 133), add two lids: `<div aria-hidden class="eye-lid eye-lid-top">` and `<div aria-hidden class="eye-lid eye-lid-bottom">`. Each is `position:absolute; inset-inline:0; height:52%; background: var(--color-paper-edge)` with the `.paper-grain` class; top one `top:0; transform: translateY(-101%)`, bottom one `bottom:0; transform: translateY(101%)`. They sit inside the existing clip, so the torn outline masks them for free.
- Give the hero section a class and trigger with CSS only, no JS:
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) { .hero:has(input[name="url"]:focus-visible) .eye-lid-top { animation: lid-top 380ms var(--ease-paper) 1; } .hero:has(input[name="url"]:focus-visible) .eye-lid-bottom { animation: lid-bottom 380ms var(--ease-paper) 1; } }`
  with `@keyframes lid-top { 0%,100% { transform: translateY(-101%) } 45%,55% { transform: translateY(0) } }` and the mirror for the bottom lid.
- Cost: two DOM nodes, two keyframes, compositor-only. The LCP element (the sky) is untouched. Reduced motion: nothing happens.
- Do not loop it, do not run it on load, do not add it to the other eyes. Once, on intent.

## Ten-rule checklist for future pages

1. **One blue action per screen.** Solid `accent-600` fill is reserved for the single primary action in view. Toggles, tabs, check marks, numerals and icon buttons are ink, paper or hairline.
2. **The page's job is on the first screen.** At 1280x720 and 360x740 the main control or content (URL field, prices, first form field, score) starts above the fold. Decorative height gives way first.
3. **No two neighbouring sections share a skeleton.** Kicker + serif heading + paragraph may open at most two sections in a row; after that change the opener (no kicker, heading only, a list, a number, a full-bleed image).
4. **No icon tiles, and no box inside a box.** No icon in a rounded square. A white card may not contain another filled rounded box; use a hairline, a left rule or a divider instead. Pills are for status and for the one eyebrow per page, not for labels.
5. **Type has seven sizes.** Display xl, lg, md, sm, title-lg, title for serif; 15 and 17 px for body; 12 px minimum for anything read. No arbitrary `text-[...]` serif sizes. Adjacent heading levels differ by at least 1.3x.
6. **Measure is 45 to 70 characters.** Body and intro text carry a `ch`-based max width (`max-w-[60ch]`, centred text `max-w-[52ch]`). Every serif heading has `text-balance`.
7. **Money and scores are set with care.** Prices go through `PriceText`; the figure is separated from "From" and "per ..." and aligned in a fixed-width, right-aligned box; the same figure never appears at two competing sizes in one panel.
8. **A collage piece needs a reason and a caption.** One fragment per composition outside the home hero, chosen for the page's subject, with a one-line figure caption. A tear marks a change of material (photo, night, footer), not every background change. Never two torn edges facing each other.
9. **Long content collapses by default.** More than six items of the same kind (issues, checks, FAQs, features) show the important ones and put the rest in a native `<details>`. Anything over three screens long gets in-page navigation that also works on phones.
10. **Motion is rare and cheap.** At most one reveal per section, on an object rather than on text; transform and opacity only; nothing loops except the documented marquee and star layers; fixed or sticky elements must not cover a form control at any width from 360 to 1920.
