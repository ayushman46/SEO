# Market research round 3: full market reference for seenhq (2026-11-23)

Scope: seenhq (https://seenhq.vercel.app, opened 2026-11-23) is a free SEO + GEO audit (homepage plus up to five pages, PageSpeed data, scores, top three issues free, full list for an email) with fixed-price human services: $49 full audit, fix sprint from $199, monitoring $29/month, AI fact-check from $69, local AI visibility setup $149 per location, AI crawler unblock $149. The site states it does not track what AI engines say and gives no outcome guarantees.

This document builds on `MARKET_RESEARCH.md` (round 1, services) and `MARKET_RESEARCH_2_PRODUCT.md` (round 2, product features). It does not repeat their findings; where it relies on them it says "R1" or "R2".

## How to read this document

- Every figure carries a source URL and the date of the source. Unless stated otherwise, the page was opened on 2026-11-23.
- Source labels: **[I]** independent (research body, regulator, platform's own documentation, news reporting, academic paper); **[V]** vendor study or vendor-published claim (the publisher sells a related product); **[S]** seen only in a search-result summary, the page itself could not be opened (HTTP 403 or similar), so treat as unverified.
- "Absent" in tables means "not described on the page opened that day". It is weaker than proof of absence.
- "Not found" means I searched and found no usable evidence.
- Pages that refused to load: marketsandmarkets.com, sparktoro.com, searchengineland.com, emarketer.com, businesswire.com, marketingweek.com, indiehackers.com, capterra.com, goodfirms.co, seoptimer.com, yourstory.com, pubsonline.informs.org, moz.com. Anything from those sites is marked [S].
- No number in this document is my own estimate unless it says "my arithmetic" or "my estimate".

## 1. Market definition and size

### 1.1 Definitions

- **SEO software and services**: tools and labour that improve visibility in classic search results (rank tracking, site audit, keyword and link data, local listings, content).
- **GEO / AEO / AI visibility**: tools and labour that affect or measure whether AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) can read, cite, mention or recommend a business. Meticulous Research's scope note is the tightest published definition found: it excludes agencies without their own software, general SEO tools without answer-engine monitoring, and the answer engines themselves (https://www.meticulousresearch.com/product/generative-engine-optimization-geo-software-market-7095, October 2026) [I, paid-report publisher].
- seenhq sits across both: an audit tool (software) plus productised services (labour).

**Method caveats (important).**
1. All of the above are paid-report publishers whose free pages do not show their method. None is a named top-tier analyst (Gartner, Forrester, IDC). I found no Gartner or IDC sizing of GEO: **not found**.
2. The USD 85-97 bn "SEO software" figures are not credible as a measure of SEO tooling. Adobe paid about USD 1.9 bn in total equity value for Semrush, one of the largest pure SEO software vendors, in a deal announced 2025-11-19 and closed 2026-04-28 (https://guptadeepak.com/who-is-buying-building-ai-visibility/, 2026-10-06, secondary). A market where a leading vendor is worth under USD 2 bn is not a USD 97 bn software market; those reports are measuring a much broader digital-marketing basket (Precedence's own segmentation confirms this). Use them only as a direction-of-travel signal.
3. The GEO figures span USD 0.37 bn (software only) to USD 1.8 bn (services) for 2026, a 5x spread driven by definition. The only defensible statement is: GEO software in 2026 is in the low hundreds of millions of dollars and services are roughly in the low single billions, with wide error bars.
4. 61% of GEO services spend is attributed to large enterprises (Coherent). seenhq's segments (startups, local businesses) are the minority of today's spend.

## 2. Demand drivers with numbers

### 2.1 AI assistant usage

Independent / platform-reported:
- 49% of US adults use AI chatbots, up from 33% in 2024; 24% daily. ChatGPT 44%, Gemini 24%, Copilot 17%, Claude 6%. 42% use chatbots to search for information. Pew Research Center, 5,119 US adults, fielded 2026-02-17 to 02-23, published 2026-06-17, https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/ [I].
- ChatGPT: over 900 m weekly active users in February 2026 and "on the verge" of 1 bn in late July 2026 per The Information's reporting of internal figures (https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-1-billion-weekly-active-users-openai-target, 2026-07-29) [I, secondary]. Later 1 bn / 1.2 bn claims are [S] and conflict on dates.
- Google AI Mode: over 1 bn monthly active users, query volume roughly doubling each quarter, queries about three times as long as classic searches (Google, 2026-05-19, https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-us-insights/) [I, platform's own figure].
- Gemini app: over 950 m monthly users on the Q2 2026 earnings call, up from 750 m in February (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/google-closes-in-on-another-billion-user-product-with-gemini/, 2026-07-23) [I].
- India: about 100 m weekly active ChatGPT users per Sam Altman, then second to the US (https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/, 2026-02-15) [I]; later reported as the largest market (R1, Inc42 2026-11-03). ChatGPT Go was launched in India under $5 and then made free for a year (same TechCrunch article).

### 2.2 AI Overviews and AI Mode prevalence

- AI Overviews went from 15% to 43% of searches in about a year; AI Mode visits rose from 126 m (June 2025) to 279 m (May 2026). Similarweb data reported by TechCrunch, 2026-07-27, https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/googles-ai-search-is-rapidly-becoming-the-default-new-data-shows/ [V data, I reporting]. Note the gap between Similarweb's 279 m "visits" and Google's "1 bn monthly users": different metrics, not reconciled.
- By intent (53 brands, 5.47 m queries): AI Overviews on 36% of informational, 8% of commercial, 5% of transactional queries; but query shape matters: comparison 95.4%, review 86.3%, question 85.9%, price/cost/buy 83.4%, "best of" 81.3%, "near me" 76.9%. Seer Interactive, 2026-04-24, https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update [V: agency selling GEO services]. The two sets of percentages come from different sub-samples and are not directly comparable.
- Local: on plain local-intent queries AI Overviews appeared on 15% and the Local Pack on 93%; on informational queries 92% vs 6%. Whitespark study as cited by Entrepreneur, 2026-10-23, https://entrepreneur.com/business-news/tech/ai-hasnt-cracked-local-search-yet-heres-why-thats-an-opportunity-for-local-businesses [V study, I reporting]. This is the strongest counter-signal for the local segment: classic local search is still mostly the map pack.

### 2.3 Click impact

- With an AI Overview present, the top-ranking page's click-through rate is an estimated 58% lower (300,000 keywords, December 2023 vs December 2025, desktop, modelled counterfactual, correlational). Ahrefs, 2026-02-04, https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/ [V].
- Organic CTR on queries with an AI Overview: 3.19% (Jan 2025) to 1.31% (Dec 2025), then 2.33% and 2.36% in Jan and Feb 2026. Being cited in the overview gives about 120% more organic clicks per impression than not being cited, but still about 38% fewer than when no overview shows. Seer Interactive (URL above) [V]. The authors warn two months is not a recovery and that the result is correlational.

### 2.4 Zero-click share

- 68.01% of US Google searches ended without a click in January to April 2026, up from 60.45% in 2024; only 0.34% of searches moved into AI Mode in that period. SparkToro on Similarweb panel data; opened via a secondary summary because sparktoro.com returned 403: https://www.techwyse.com/news/reports-data/google-zero-click-searches-68-percent-2026 (2026-06-11) [I study, secondary source]. Caveats: assumes two-thirds mobile, excludes the Google mobile app, and does not isolate the effect of AI Overviews. EU figure: **not found**.

### 2.5 Referral traffic from AI assistants

- All AI chatbots together sent 0.28% of total web traffic (3.5 m visitors) across 74,752 sites in March 2026; ChatGPT 2.7 m, Perplexity and Gemini about 230,000 each, Claude about 102,000. Google's share fell from 35.11% (June 2025) to 30.53%. Ahrefs, 2026-05-15, https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/ [V]. The page is internally inconsistent on Google's March share (28.12% vs 30.53%).
- ChatGPT answers that included citations: 6.8% of US desktop queries in May 2026; share of ChatGPT visits that led to a webpage referral rose from 25% (March 2026) to nearly 60% by 2026-05-30 after a search update on May 7 (Similarweb via TechCrunch, URL in 2.2) [V data].
- AI crawler mix (Cloudflare Radar via SEOmator's September 2026 report, data pulled 2026-10-10, https://seomator.com/blog/ai-crawler-report) [V reporting of I data]: GPTBot's share of AI-bot traffic fell from 12.6% (Jan) to 6.2% (Sep) while OAI-SearchBot rose from 1.7% to 3.6% and PerplexityBot from 0.2% to 2.2%; crawl for "search" purpose rose from 2.6% to 10.6%. Training crawl is flat; retrieval crawl is what is growing.
- Botify logs (about 7 bn log lines, enterprise sites, Nov 2024 to March 2026): GPTBot 2.9x and OAI-SearchBot 3.5x since August 2025; ChatGPT-User down 28% from December 2025. https://www.botify.com/blog/openai-tripled-web-crawl (2026-04-23) [V, enterprise sample].

### 2.7 Marketer survey data

- 78% of 600 marketing professionals say their company funds GEO; monthly GEO budget is under $500 for 25% and $500 to $2,000 for 32% (57% under $2,000); 63% plan to increase GEO focus in 12 months, 7% to reduce; 9% do not measure it at all. Clutch via Pollfish, fielded August 2025, page updated 2026-05-20, https://clutch.co/resources/geo-vs-seo-ppc-survey-report [V: Clutch is an agency marketplace].
- 66% of 494 agency professionals report increased client demand for AEO / SEO for AI search (the top category); 64% name AI Overviews as a top concern; 11% cite AI search results as a new-business source. AgencyAnalytics, fielded Feb to April 2026, published 2026-06-30, https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/agency-benchmarks-2026-ai-search [V].
- 67% of Fiverr's surveyed search freelancers saw GEO requests rise in the past year; 61% say clients expect lower prices because AI is involved; 61% report typical project costs of $150 to $300. Fiverr Holiday Marketing Index via Search Engine Journal, 2026-09-22, https://www.searchenginejournal.com/fiverr-survey-search-freelancers-geo-demand/590403/ [V; sample "hundreds", undisclosed]. Same article: 43% of State of Search 2027 respondents plan to prioritise GEO.
- 94% of 250+ enterprise executives plan to increase AEO/GEO investment in 2026 (Conductor) [S, V].
- 82% of 1,000+ B2B software buyers sourced software recommendations from an AI chatbot in the last 24 months; review sites (38%) edged AI chatbots (37%) as the top shortlist influence. G2, released 2026-07-22, https://company.g2.com/news/buyer-behavior-2026 [V: G2 sells review profiles]. Earlier G2 post: half of buyers start in an AI chatbot rather than Google (https://learn.g2.com/ai-search-surging-for-b2b-buyers, 2025-10-06) [V].
- Measurement is unreliable: brand overlap between API answers and scraped consumer-UI answers was 15.5% to 23.8% (1,000 prompts, 5 products, collected 2026-08-04); source-domain overlap about 12-13% for most engines. Surfer, updated 2026-10-04, https://surfer.ai/blog/llm-scraped-ai-answers-vs-api-results [V: sells a scraping-based tracker; one sample per prompt]. Combined with BrightLocal's 21-33% repeat-run overlap (R1; re-opened: https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/, 2026-09-16), any "visibility score" has large noise.

### 2.8 SMB survey data (what they do not know)

- Nearly 60% of 1,041 small-business owners (North America, UK, Australia) "don't know where to start" with AI search optimisation; over two-thirds have not looked at how they appear in AI answers; more than half have not optimised at all; of those who checked, 40% found incorrect information; 34% have used no AI tool for marketing or SEO. Squarespace, fielded April 2026, https://www.squarespace.com/blog/ai-search-for-business-owners [V; 538 of the respondents are Squarespace users].
- 13% of small businesses are actively optimising for AI search; 31% know it exists but do not know where to start; 22% are hearing of it for the first time; 78% want to see how competitors appear in AI results. Bluehost, https://www.bluehost.com/state-of-small-business-ai-confidence/ai-search-readiness [V; sample size, dates and method not stated on the page].
- About one-third of 300+ SMBs have no website (56% of solopreneurs); 50% manage SEO in-house; 48% say they are very or extremely familiar with GEO; about half monitor AI referrals. WordStream/LocaliQ, updated 2026-08-17, https://www.wordstream.com/blog/smb-website-trends-report-2026 [V]. Note the conflict with Bluehost's 13%: different samples and question wording.
- 56% of SMBs cite measuring ROI from AI visibility as a challenge (eMarketer) [S].
- R1 figures still apply: BrightLocal (46% of 1,002 owners have not checked), Small Business Expo (14.8% saw wrong information).
- SMB budgets for GEO specifically, and stated intent to buy a GEO audit at a given price: **not found**. The Clutch brackets above are for companies with marketing staff, not owner-run businesses.

### 2.9 Demand counter-evidence (keep on file)

- Google's own guidance says there are "no additional technical requirements" for AI Overviews and AI Mode beyond being indexed and snippet-eligible, and no special schema or AI text file is needed (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features, no date on page) [I].
- First-party measurement is now free: Search Console's generative AI performance reports (impressions, pages, countries, devices; announced 2026-06-03, worldwide by 2026-08-31, https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) [I]; Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance (citations, grounding queries, page-level counts; 2026-02-10, https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) [I].
- A controlled Vercel experiment found that adding llms.txt, JSON-LD, sitemaps or Markdown mirrors did not change whether agents could retrieve an answer already present in server-rendered HTML (https://vercel.com/kb/guide/agent-readability-spec, 2026-08-28) [V: sells hosting, but the finding cuts against selling extras].
- AI referrals are about 0.28% of traffic (2.5).

## 3. Customer segments

Prices "paid today" are market prices from the sources given. Willingness-to-pay (WTP) evidence at seenhq's exact price points is untested in every segment; this is the largest unknown in the whole document.

### 3.1 Solo founders and indie SaaS

- **Job to be done:** "When a buyer asks an assistant for tools like mine, be named, and be described correctly (price, features)."
- **Current alternatives:** free graders (HubSpot AI Search Grader, Semrush free checker at 3 runs/day with no signup, https://www.semrush.com/free-tools/ai-search-visibility-checker/; Scrunch free site audit; Cloudflare isitagentready; Vercel is-agentic.com), asking the assistants by hand, low-tier trackers.
- **What they pay today:** Otterly Lite EUR 29/month for 16 prompts (https://otterly.ai/pricing); Ahrefs Starter $29/month; Seobility free plan with 3 tracked AI prompts (https://www.seobility.net/en/pricing/); AthenaHQ free tier with 300 credits (https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).
- **WTP evidence:** weak. Indie Hackers threads exist ("why your indie SaaS is invisible to ChatGPT", "I scanned 35 SaaS products...") but the pages returned 403 [S]; one founder claims about 14% of sign-ups from assistants [S, single anecdote]. Ahrefs' own self-attributed sign-ups (2.6) show assistants can matter for a SaaS. A survey of indie founders' spend on GEO: **not found**.
- **How they buy:** self-serve, card, after a free result; from communities (Indie Hackers, X, Product Hunt); they prefer a prompt they can paste into a coding agent over a service (R2 item 4).
- **What stops them:** they can do it themselves; AI traffic is tiny in their analytics; distrust of scores given known noise (2.7).

### 3.2 Funded startups (seed to Series B)

- **Job:** "Make sure assistants shortlist us and state our pricing and capabilities correctly, and show the board a number."
- **Alternatives:** Peec ($95/$245/$495), Scrunch Core $250, AthenaHQ Starter $295, Promptwatch $95/$245/$579, Semrush Starter $199 (monthly) with 50 prompts, in-house content/SEO hire, GEO agency retainers.
- **What they pay today:** $95 to $500/month for tracking; agency Growth tier $4,500 to $11,000/month with 6-month minimum; one-time audits $1,500 to $12,000, median $4,300 (First Page Sage, 1,146 quotes from 214 agencies, 2026-07-06 to 10-02, https://firstpagesage.com/reports/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/) [V: an agency].
- **WTP evidence:** strongest of all segments. 82% of B2B buyers used a chatbot for software recommendations (G2, 2.7); Peec passed $10 m ARR by June 2026 [S]; fact-checking is now a paid feature on Peec Pro ($245: 10 fact-checked prompts), Scrunch Core (100 fact-checked pages) and Promptwatch Business ($579), which shows buyers pay for accuracy, but only as part of a subscription.
- **How they buy:** marketing lead or founder, free trial then monthly card; compare 3 tools from listicles; want Looker Studio / API / Slack.
- **What stops them:** tool sprawl, noisy data, no clear fix path ("diagnoses but does not fix" is the complaint levelled even at Shopify's free scanner, see 5.3 gap N3).

### 3.3 Local single-location businesses

- **Job:** "Be the business the assistant names for 'best X near me', with the right hours, phone and address."
- **Alternatives:** Google Business Profile (free), BrightLocal ($41/$54/$65 monthly, USA/UK/Canada/Australia only, https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/), Localo ($39/month annual, any country, https://localo.com/pricing), Local Falcon (from $24.99/month), Whitespark ($1/location/month platform; listings service $20 to $999 one-time, https://whitespark.ca/pricing/), a local freelancer.
- **What they pay today:** local SEO averages $1,557/month from providers, freelancers about $1,150 (Ahrefs survey of 439 providers, page updated 2024-08-15, https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing) [V, old]; GEO Starter retainers for local and small businesses $1,800 to $4,200/month, 3-month minimum (First Page Sage). Many pay nothing: about a third of SMBs have no website (WordStream).
- **WTP evidence:** indirect. 45% of consumers use AI for local recommendations (R1, BrightLocal); 40% of owners who checked found wrong information (Squarespace). But classic local results are still dominated by the map pack (Whitespark, 2.2), and ChatGPT recommended 1.2% of locations (R1, SOCi). No evidence that owners pay $49 to $199 one-time for AI work specifically: **not found**.
- **How they buy:** referral, a visible problem (wrong hours, lost calls), WhatsApp/phone, one-time fixed price; they do not buy dashboards.
- **What stops them:** do not know where to start (about 60%, Squarespace); no time; fear of being upsold; cannot judge quality; "I already have Google".
- **Useful fact for delivery:** where assistants get local facts differs by engine. Yext, 491,972 query-market cases, Feb to Aug 2026 (https://www.yext.com/research/articles/four-ais-four-different-answers, September 2026) [V]: Gemini cites first-party sites most (41.9%), OpenAI directories most (40.9%), Anthropic review platforms far more than others (20.5%), Perplexity evenly split. BrightLocal: GBP is 28.5% of citations, business websites 42%, Yelp 9.53%; ChatGPT rarely uses GBP [V].

### 3.4 Multi-location businesses and franchises

- **Job:** "Keep facts right for every location across every assistant and report it to head office."
- **Alternatives:** Yext, Uberall, SOCi, Birdeye (enterprise, contact sales; Yext's pricing page returned 404), BrightLocal multi-location (Track is $62/month for 2-5 locations up to $524/month for 91-100).
- **What they pay:** per-location SaaS plus managed services (BrightLocal managed local SEO from $499/month). Enterprise list prices: **not found** (contact sales).
- **WTP evidence:** vendors report 20% of locations invisible in ChatGPT scans (Birdeye) [S, V] and 83% of restaurants invisible (Uberall) [S, V]. Budget surveys: **not found**.
- **How they buy:** procurement, annual contract, integrations, SLAs.
- **What stops seenhq:** this is not a fit. A team of a few people cannot meet procurement, SSO and per-location data requirements. Treat 2-5 location owners as "local" and ignore the rest.

### 3.5 E-commerce stores

- **Job:** "Have my products appear, with the right price and stock, when a shopper asks an assistant, and be buyable there."
- **Alternatives:** Shopify Agentic Storefronts (products discoverable in ChatGPT by default for Shopify merchants; channels: ChatGPT, Copilot, AI Mode, Gemini app; non-Shopify brands can join via the Agentic plan; https://shopify.com/news/agentic-commerce-momentum, 2026-03-24) [V]; Shopify's free readiness scanner (31 checks, any public storefront per the post, https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-launched-a-free-agentic-commerce-readiness-scanner-here-is-what-it-found-and-what-to-do-next/614767, 2026-04-26); Fudge free checker (https://www.fudge.ai/tools/shopify-ai-readiness-checker/); AisleReady (free score, $49 fix report); Shopify apps; Peec AI Shopping on all plans; feed managers (Lengow etc.).

- **WTP evidence:** 11% of small and medium merchants are "agent-ready", 15% have the structured product data agents need, 23% can tell AI traffic from human traffic (attributed to PYMNTS/Visa in https://forkast.news/67-of-retailers-say-they-plan-to-open-catalogs-to-ai-agents-fewer-than-a-handful-have-actually-done-it/, 2026-10-24; sample not stated, attribution implied) [secondary, weak]. 58% of shoppers have used AI to research products, 17% are comfortable buying through AI (ChannelEngine via the same article). Shopify says AI-driven traffic to its stores grew 8x and orders 13x YoY in Q1 2026 [S, V].
- **How they buy:** app store, one-click install, free plan first.
- **What stops them:** on Shopify the platform does most of it for free; off Shopify the path is new: OpenAI's onboarding now runs through Business Hub claim and identity verification, then a feed by upload, hosted URL or SFTP (https://developers.openai.com/commerce/product-feeds/onboarding) and "the standard OpenAI-format upload currently targets the US" (https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products) [I]. Instant Checkout launched US-only (https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/16/openai-expands-agentic-commerce-push/, 2026-02-16).

Useful as a public example of "you blocked the crawler yourself".
- **How they buy:** WhatsApp, phone, UPI, one-time price, referral; founders via X/LinkedIn.
- **What stops them:** price sensitivity, no website, language, trust in remote vendors, GST invoicing needs. Share of Indian AI queries in Hindi or regional languages: **not found**.

A user with a coding agent can already pull audit data into it. The "copy as one prompt" export is still simpler for a non-subscriber, but it is no longer novel.
- **Fact checking is moving down-market**, from No Hacks at $1,000+ (R1) to Peec Pro at $245/month, Scrunch Core at $250/month and Promptwatch Business at $579/month. The low one-time price is still open; expect it to close within a year.
- **Crawler unblock has a public Indian example**: IndiaMART had blocked ChatGPT's crawler while suing over absence from ChatGPT (3.7).

### 5.3 New gaps (not in rounds 1 or 2)

Build convention: "crawler + deterministic code + one LLM call" as asked. Effort is my estimate in engineering hours for a working first version inside the existing audit engine, excluding design polish.

#### N1. Snippet-eligibility and AI opt-out check
- **What:** flag anything that makes a page ineligible for Google AI Overviews / AI Mode or strips it from Bing/Copilot answers: `noindex`, `nosnippet`, `max-snippet:0` or very low values, `data-nosnippet` wrapped around core facts, `X-Robots-Tag` headers, Bing `NOARCHIVE`/`NOCACHE`, and `Google-Extended` disallow (explained as "does not affect Search AI features, does affect Gemini grounding").
- **Demand evidence:** Google's documentation states the only requirement for AI features is being indexed and eligible for a snippet, and lists `nosnippet`, `data-nosnippet`, `max-snippet`, `noindex` as the controls (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) [I]. Microsoft documents page-level directives rather than a Copilot robots token (secondary, https://eseospace.com/blog/bing-webmaster-tools-indexnow-ai-search/, 2026-10-13). Owner complaints specifically about this: **not found**. This is a requirement-driven check, not a complaint-driven one.
- **Who lacks it (pages opened):** HubSpot AEO Grader (no crawl), Semrush free checker (no crawl), Cloudflare isitagentready (protocol checks), Otterly (crawlability and content audit, directive detail not described), Seobility (robots.txt AI-bot check only). Classic suites flag `noindex` generally but none of the opened pages ties snippet directives to AI eligibility.
- **Build:** crawler already has HTML and headers; parse meta robots, googlebot/bingbot-specific metas, `X-Robots-Tag`, `data-nosnippet` spans; intersect with the facts found by the existing fact extractor (the one LLM call the audit already makes). No extra LLM call.
- **Effort:** 3-4 hours.
- **Honest limits:** being eligible does not mean being shown; Search Console's own AI opt-out setting (reported in secondary coverage, not opened) is not visible from outside; `max-snippet` thresholds that matter are not published.

#### N2. White-label, pay-per-report audit for freelancers and small agencies
- **What:** the existing audit report with the reseller's logo, colours and contact line, as a share link and PDF, priced per report or as a low flat monthly fee, with an explicit "we never contact your client" term.
- **Demand evidence:** 66% of 494 agency professionals report rising client demand for AEO (https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/agency-benchmarks-2026-ai-search, 2026-06-30) [V]; 67% of Fiverr search freelancers report more GEO requests and 61% say a typical project is $150-$300 (https://www.searchenginejournal.com/fiverr-survey-search-freelancers-geo-demand/590403/, 2026-09-22) [V]; 35.6% of surveyed SEO agencies are solo and 42.5% charge under $1,000/month (https://sitechecker.pro/seo-agency-growth-data-study-2026/) [V].
- **Who lacks it at this price:** Insites from $399/month plus per-audit fees; SE Ranking white label only through a $69/month annual add-on on a $129+ plan; AgencyAnalytics $20/client/month and its AI Tracker is tracking, not a readiness audit; Otterly offers only branded Looker reports to partners; Promptwatch white label is Enterprise. Closest low-price rival: Airefs agency plan $49-$59/month with own-logo reports (a tracker, not a site audit). SEOptimer (the classic white-label audit tool) could not be opened: **unverified**.
- **Build:** branding parameters on the report template; a reseller account table; Stripe/Razorpay credits; deterministic. No LLM call beyond the audit's own.
- **Effort:** 12-18 hours including billing.
- **Honest limits:** R2 found share links and PDFs are free elsewhere (SEOmator), so the value is the audit content plus the reseller terms, not the PDF. Channel conflict with seenhq's own services must be ruled out in writing. WTP is inferred, not tested.

#### N3. Product-page shopping readiness for stores off Shopify, with an OpenAI-format feed draft
- **What:** for a product URL or a sample of up to 20 product pages: check the nine required feed fields (`item_id`, `title`, `description`, `url`, `brand`, `seller_name`, `image_url`, `availability`, `price`) plus identifiers (`gtin`, `mpn`), returns and shipping fields can be filled from the page; check that price, stock and SKU agree between visible HTML, JSON-LD and (if supplied) the feed; flag `AggregateRating` markup with no visible reviews; flag schema injected only by JavaScript; then export a draft JSONL/CSV in OpenAI's field names for the owner to complete.
- **Demand evidence:** OpenAI's feed reference and onboarding path (https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products; https://developers.openai.com/commerce/product-feeds/onboarding) [I]; merchants in a Shopify Community thread (2026-08-07 to 11-04) ask for exactly these consistency, variant, GTIN and Bing-index checks (https://community.shopify.com/t/free-tool-to-check-if-your-products-show-up-in-chatgpt-and-ai-search-what-am-i-missing/661996) [I, forum]; 11% of small and medium merchants "agent-ready" (PYMNTS/Visa via forkast.news, weak attribution); the criticism that Shopify's scanner "does not fix anything" (community post, from a competing app author).
- **Who lacks it:** Shopify's scanner diagnoses only; Fudge asks assistants but has no feed, Merchant Center or variant checks; AisleReady and the Apify actor score schema but create no feed; Peec tracks shopping results but does not audit pages; Otterly and Semrush pricing pages list no product-page audit. One independent builder in the thread already maps to OpenAI feed fields, so this is **not** unique.
- **Build:** crawler fetches product pages; deterministic JSON-LD/microdata parser, GTIN check-digit validation, visible-price regex against schema price; one LLM call to extract attributes (material, size, returns window) from page text into feed fields; serialiser to JSONL.
- **Effort:** 14-20 hours.
- **Honest limits:** the standard OpenAI-format upload currently targets the US; submitting a feed requires the merchant to claim and verify in Business Hub; how ChatGPT weighs crawled pages against feeds is not documented (the thread's "Bing Shopping Graph" claim is unverified); on Shopify the platform already does this for free, so the audience is WooCommerce, custom and Indian D2C stores selling to the US. No headless browser means client-rendered prices are reported as "missing in raw HTML".

#### N4. Review and reputation signal check (site side)
- **What:** detect whether the site links to claimed profiles on review platforms relevant to its type (Google, Yelp, Trustpilot, G2, Capterra, BBB; Justdial/Practo for India as listing checks only), whether review markup matches visible reviews, and show a per-engine "where your reviews are read" note.
- **Demand evidence:** Trustpilot/SEER study of 800,000+ AI responses: brands with no claimed Trustpilot profile were cited 1% of the time, 54% with a claimed profile (median 13 reviews), 75% with an active profile (median 81) (https://business.trustpilot.com/guides-reports/get-seen-in-search/what-ai-says-about-your-brand, 2026) [V: Trustpilot sells profiles; "cited" here means Trustpilot being cited]; Yext: Anthropic cites review platforms for 20.5% of local sources vs 1.7-1.9% for Gemini and OpenAI [V]; G2 shortlist influence (review sites 38%) [V]. Counter-evidence: across 500 G2 categories reviews explained under 2% of variance in citations, R-squared 0.009 (https://learn.g2.com/do-more-g2-reviews-mean-more-ai-visibility, 2025-10-23) [V]; BrightLocal's local study did not measure reviews as a factor.
- **Who lacks it:** AI trackers opened (Otterly, Peec, Promptwatch) list citation-source tracking but no site-side review audit; free graders (HubSpot, Semrush, Cloudflare, Vercel) none. BrightLocal Grow and Localo manage reviews but BrightLocal is limited to four countries.
- **Build:** crawler collects outbound links and `sameAs`; deterministic domain match against a platform list by business type; JSON-LD `AggregateRating` vs visible review count; no scraping of the review platforms themselves; the audit's existing LLM call classifies business type.
- **Effort:** 6-8 hours.
- **Honest limits:** we do not read review counts from the platforms (terms and blocking); evidence that reviews cause AI recommendations is correlational and vendor-run; for Indian clinics, review solicitation is restricted (R1, NMC guidelines), so the check must be informational only.

#### N5. Bing index and IndexNow readiness
- **What:** check for Bing Webmaster verification (`msvalidate.01` meta or BingSiteAuth.xml), sitemap with fresh `lastmod`, IndexNow key file reference, and give the owner a one-click IndexNow key and submission snippet plus a link to Bing's AI Performance report.
- **Demand evidence:** Microsoft says AI Performance covers "Microsoft Copilot, AI-generated summaries in Bing, and select partner integrations" and that IndexNow "helps AI systems reference the current version of a page" (https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview, 2026-02-10) [I]; merchants in the Shopify thread ask for Bing indexing and Bing Webmaster checks [I, forum]. Counter-evidence: OpenAI does not name Bing as a search provider and no OpenAI statement links IndexNow to ChatGPT visibility (eseospace summary, 2026-10-13); ChatGPT citations overlapped Bing's top 10 by about 8% in July 2025 (https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/, 2025-08-11, 15,000 prompts) [V, older than 12 months].
- **Who lacks it:** none of the free graders or tracker pricing pages opened mention a Bing verification or IndexNow check. Whether Semrush/Ahrefs site audits include it: **unverified**.
- **Build:** deterministic; fetch homepage meta, `/BingSiteAuth.xml`, sitemap; generate a key and instructions. No LLM call.
- **Effort:** 5-7 hours.
- **Honest limits:** we cannot see whether Bing has actually indexed the site without querying Bing, which its terms restrict; IndexNow keys can live anywhere, so "not found" is weak; benefit for ChatGPT is unconfirmed and must be worded as "Bing and Copilot".

#### N6. HTTP correctness for agents (parity add)
- **What:** soft-404 detection (random path returns 200), redirect chain length, `429` without `Retry-After`, `Accept: text/markdown` response type and `Vary: Accept`.
- **Demand evidence:** Vercel's agent readability spec (URL above); Mintlify's 2,400-test benchmark: failed requests per task 2.23 for HTML vs 0.11 for Markdown plus llms.txt, accuracy 94-99% regardless of format (https://www.mintlify.com/state-of-knowledge/2026) [V]; agents were 66% of Mintlify-hosted docs traffic in August 2026 [V, own platform].
- **Who lacks it:** free elsewhere (is-agentic.com, isitagentready). This is not a differentiator. It matters only for dev-tool startups' docs.
- **Build:** four extra requests per site; deterministic.
- **Effort:** 4-5 hours.
- **Honest limits:** Vercel's own experiment found mirrors and discovery files did not change retrieval when the answer was already in server-rendered HTML; label as low impact.

#### N7. Language coverage check (multilingual and Indic)
- **What:** report `lang`, valid reciprocal `hreflang`, and whether core facts (name, services, prices, hours, address) exist in each declared language and, for Indian sites, in Hindi or the declared regional language; flag machine-translated-looking pages only as "review needed".
- **Demand evidence:** models prefer citing documents in the query language (arXiv 2509.13930, "Linguistic Nepotism") [S, abstract-level]; in a 320-response test, local (Lebanese) sources were 31.6% of citations for English questions and 56.7% for Arabic (https://hackernoon.com/your-language-changes-who-ai-search-cites, 2026-10-22) [I, single author, one run per question]; Wikipedia leads in 11 of 12 query languages across 167,551 citations, 82.4% of citations go to sites not associated with the brand (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25787, v2 2026-11-15) [I]. India-specific share of AI queries by language: **not found**; Gemini supports nine Indian languages [S].
- **Who lacks it:** Pallix's launch material mentions no Indian-language support; Otterly and Peec track prompts in any language but do not audit site language coverage; free graders none.
- **Build:** deterministic `lang`/`hreflang` validation and Unicode-script ratio per page; one LLM call comparing the extracted fact list across language versions.
- **Effort:** 6-8 hours.
- **Honest limits:** evidence is not India-specific; the team must be able to read any language it reports on (R2 rule); translation is not sold as a guaranteed gain.

#### N8. Entity and `sameAs` / Wikidata check
- **What:** check Organization/LocalBusiness schema for `sameAs`; resolve any Wikidata ID through Wikidata's public API and compare name and URL; verify that linked profiles link back.
- **Demand evidence:** Wikipedia is the top-cited domain in 11 of 12 languages (arXiv above) [I]; Wikipedia page views were the strongest predictor of LLM visibility in a Seer study of 27 athletes, rho 0.810 (https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/what-ai-thinks-about-your-brand-is-already-written, 2026-04-27) [V; athletes, not businesses]. Direct evidence that a Wikidata item raises AI citations for small businesses: **not found**.
- **Who lacks it:** mainstream trackers and graders opened do not describe it. A dedicated free-looking tool exists (https://jwatte.com/blog/blog-tool-knowledge-graph-wikidata-audit/, pricing not stated).
- **Build:** deterministic JSON-LD parse, Wikidata API call, back-link fetch. No LLM call.
- **Effort:** 5-6 hours.
- **Honest limits:** most local businesses and early startups do not meet Wikipedia notability; creating thin Wikidata items risks deletion; present as hygiene, lowest priority.

#### N9. Change alerts
- **What:** weekly re-crawl; email or WhatsApp message when AI-crawler status codes, robots/meta directives, or any core fact (price, phone, hours) change; includes "your CDN default changed" detection.
- **Demand evidence:** R1: Search Engine Journal on the absence of alerts for wrong AI answers; Cloudflare's 2026-09-15 default change; WP Engine platform-level 429s. This round: Otterly and Peec pricing pages mention no alerts; Writesonic lists alerts only on Enterprise.
- **Build:** needs the database (R2 #8); diff of stored audit JSON; deterministic; no LLM call unless summarising the diff.
- **Effort:** 10-14 hours after the database exists.
- **Honest limits:** alerts cover the site, not what assistants say; false alarms from A/B tests and call-tracking numbers.

#### N10. Forum footprint (Reddit) note
- **What:** tell the owner whether their category's AI answers lean on forums and how to check their own presence by hand.
- **Demand evidence:** reddit.com is 18.1% of top-source citations in Google AI Mode, YouTube 17.0%, Wikipedia 5.9% (https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-mode/, updated 2026-11-03) [V]; share varies widely by engine and vertical (Tinuiti, Marketing Week) [S].
- **Who has it:** Promptwatch, ReddGrow ($99/month), Airefs brand mentions from $299/month [S for the first two].
- **Build:** no automated Reddit fetching (Reddit is litigating against scrapers, see 7.3); a static, deterministic guidance block by business type plus search links the owner clicks.
- **Effort:** 3-4 hours for guidance only; 8-10 hours if an official Reddit API integration is approved.
- **Honest limits:** seeding forum posts is against most communities' rules and seenhq should not sell it.

#### N11. India directory consistency (Justdial first)
- **What:** owner pastes their Justdial (and Sulekha/IndiaMART) listing details or URL; compare name, phone, address, hours with the site and schema.
- **Demand evidence:** Justdial's ChatGPT app (advertorial, 2026-10-23); BrightLocal not available in India (R1).
- **Build:** deterministic comparison against the truth sheet; if the listing page cannot be fetched, compare pasted text. One LLM call to normalise addresses.
- **Effort:** 6-8 hours.
- **Honest limits:** no proof ChatGPT uses Justdial unless the user tags it; listing pages may block fetching.

#### N12. Audit API and CI check
- **What:** JSON endpoint and a GitHub Action that fails a build when AI crawlers get blocked or core facts drop out of raw HTML.
- **Demand evidence:** **not found.** Vercel already offers a free report API and MCP server.
- **Effort:** about 8 hours. Lowest priority.

## 6. Positioning options

### Option A: "The fix desk": fixed-price, human-checked fixes for small sites, below agency pricing
- **Claim:** free audit that finds what blocks AI and search from reading you, then a person fixes it for a published one-time price and re-tests.
- **Strongest evidence:** agencies start at $1,800-$4,200/month with a 3-month minimum and a $4,300 median audit (First Page Sage); nearly 60% of small-business owners do not know where to start and 40% of those who checked found wrong information (Squarespace, vendor); the standing complaint about free scanners is that they diagnose and do not fix (Shopify community); trackers moved up-market (Profound enterprise-only since September 2026).
- **Against:** freelancers already sell GEO projects at $150-$300 (Fiverr) and clients expect lower prices; OneTimeGEO sells $297-$997 packages with a money-back "citation results" guarantee that seenhq cannot honestly match; WTP at $49-$199 is untested; human hours cap revenue.

### Option B: "The readiness engine for freelancers and small agencies"
- **Claim:** the cheapest honest AI-search readiness audit a freelancer can put their logo on, per report.
- **Strongest evidence:** 66% of agencies report rising AEO demand (AgencyAnalytics); 67% of freelancers see more GEO requests (Fiverr); white-label floors are $399/month (Insites) or annual add-ons (SE Ranking); 69% of surveyed agencies charge under $2,500/month and 35.6% are solo (Sitechecker).
- **Against:** Airefs already sells unlimited-client plans at $49-$59/month; all three surveys are vendor-run; share links and PDFs are free elsewhere (R2); resellers churn and expect support.

### Option C: "India-first": rupee-priced, UPI, WhatsApp-delivered AI readiness for Indian local businesses and D2C stores
- **Claim:** the one-time, no-retainer option in India, including for owners without a website.
- **Strongest evidence:** India is ChatGPT's largest or second-largest market (TechCrunch, R1 Inc42); BrightLocal does not operate in India; local trackers start at Rs 2,499/month but are dashboards for brands and agencies (Pallix); retainers start at Rs 15,000-35,000/month (R1); Justdial now has a ChatGPT surface.
- **Against:** every price point is vendor-published and no demand survey exists (**not found**); 76% of Indian SEO providers already charge $1,000/month or less (Ahrefs, old); Hindi/regional delivery needs readers on the team; low ticket sizes; clinics are restricted (R1).

### Recommendation
Lead with **Option A** as the public position, because it is the only one supported by evidence from more than one independent direction (agency price floor, owner confusion, trackers leaving the low end) and it matches what the site already says. Use **Option C as a price list and delivery mode** (INR, UPI, WhatsApp) rather than a separate brand, since the product and services are the same. Run **Option B as a small, time-boxed test** (N2 at 12-18 hours) because it is the cheapest way to get distribution without ad spend and it will produce the WTP data that is missing everywhere. Do not position on tracking or scores in any option: that layer is funded by more than $600 m of venture capital and is being given away by HubSpot and Semrush.

Wording guardrails that follow from the evidence: say "readable", "accurate" and "eligible", never "ranked", "cited" or "recommended"; quote no conversion multiples; do not claim "nobody else does this" for any feature.

## 7. Risks

### 7.1 Platform changes
- **Google gives the measurement away and says no special work is needed.** Search Console generative AI reports are worldwide since 2026-08-31; Google's guidance says no extra technical requirements, schema or AI files. Impact: weakens any "GEO-specific" scoring; strengthens fixes that are also good SEO.
- **Platforms absorb readiness.** Shopify makes products discoverable in ChatGPT by default; Wix auto-generates llms.txt (R2); Cloudflare and Vercel publish free agent-readiness scanners. Impact: every check that a host can automate will be automated.
- **Defaults flip under customers.** Cloudflare's 2026-09-15 default (R1); OpenAI's crawler mix is shifting from training (GPTBot down from 12.6% to 6.2% of AI-bot traffic) to retrieval (OAI-SearchBot up from 1.7% to 3.6%), so per-bot policies go stale. Impact: favours monitoring (N9).
- **Programmes are US-first.** OpenAI's standard feed upload targets the US; Business Hub local claims are US-only (R1); Instant Checkout launched US-only. Impact: limits what can be sold in India today.
- **Sources of truth move.** ChatGPT's referral behaviour changed sharply after a 2026-05-07 update (Similarweb via TechCrunch). Any guidance tied to one engine's behaviour can be wrong within weeks.

### 7.2 Commoditisation by free tools
- Free and near-free: HubSpot grader (and AEO at $50/month), Semrush checker (3/day, no signup), Ahrefs free checker, Scrunch free site audit, Cloudflare and Vercel scanners, Shopify scanner, Seobility free plan, Pallix free audit, AthenaHQ free tier.
- Fact-checking is moving from $1,000+ services to $245/month plans (5.2).
- MCP servers in mainstream suites reduce the value of export features.
- Mitigation is the service layer and narrow checks (R2), not more scores.

### 7.3 Legal and terms constraints on querying AI assistants
- OpenAI's terms list among prohibited acts: "Automatically or programmatically extract data or Output" (https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/, effective 2026-01-01) [I]. Consumer-UI scraping of ChatGPT is therefore a terms breach; human-typed checks (R1) and official API use are the safe routes. API answers differ from what users see (brand overlap 15.5-23.8%, Surfer), so API-based "what ChatGPT says" reports must be labelled as API results.
- Google sued SerpApi on 2025-12-19 under the DMCA for circumventing its anti-scraping system (https://blog.google/technology/safety-security/serpapi-lawsuit/) [I]; SerpApi's motion to dismiss was pending on 2026-02-21, and Reddit has a parallel suit against SerpApi and others (https://www.theregister.com/2026/02/21/serpapi_google_scraping_lawsuit/) [I]. Later rulings: **not found**. Many trackers depend on such data suppliers; seenhq should not build on scraped Google AI Overview data.
- Perplexity, Anthropic and Google consumer terms on automated querying were not opened this round: **unverified**; assume the same restriction.
- Reddit fetching: avoid without an API agreement (N10).
- seenhq's own crawler: keep honouring robots.txt, identify itself, stay SSRF-safe; simulated bot user-agents must be labelled as simulations (R1).

### 7.4 Trademark and claims risks
- **Claims:** the FTC's Operation AI Comply (since September 2024) and more than a dozen "AI washing" cases in 2025 target unsubstantiated capability claims, including business-to-business claims and materials supplied to resellers (Holland & Knight summary, https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/operation-ai-comply-2-years-later-continued-enforcement, 2026-08-18) [I, law-firm summary; no GEO-specific case found]. Guarantees such as "30-day citation results or money back" (OneTimeGEO) are the kind of claim to avoid. If N2 ships, reseller materials carry the same duty.
- **No right to visibility:** IndiaMART v. OpenAI (Calcutta High Court, 2026-05-20, via CyberPeace) means a customer cannot compel an assistant to list them; seenhq must never imply a remedy exists.
- **Trademarks:** using "ChatGPT", "Gemini", "Claude", "Perplexity" descriptively is normal practice but logos and "certified/partner" wording need permission. Whether "GEO" or "AEO" are registered marks in India or the US, and whether "seenhq" conflicts with an existing mark: **not checked; needs a trademark search**.
- **India advertising:** NMC rules for clinics (R1). ASCI guidance on AI-related advertising claims: **not found**.
- **Reviews:** no incentives or gating in review-ask kits (R1); N4 stays informational.

### 7.5 Model and measurement accuracy
- Repeat-run overlap of local AI answers is 21-33%; 71-80% of businesses appear in half of attempts or fewer (BrightLocal, 2026-09-16) [V].
- API vs UI brand overlap 15.5-23.8% (Surfer) [V].
- Most published "what drives AI visibility" studies are correlational and vendor-run (Seer, Trustpilot, G2, Yext, Insites); G2's own regression shows R-squared 0.009.
- seenhq's LLM-read facts can be wrong; every LLM-derived finding should show the source text it was read from, and deterministic checks should be preferred where both are possible.
- Simulated crawler probes come from non-verified IPs and can be wrong in either direction (R1).

### 7.6 Business risks specific to a small team
- WTP at seenhq's prices is untested in every segment (3).
- Human services at $49-$199 with 3-12 hours of work (R1 effort estimates) leave thin margins outside India-cost labour; price or scope must be watched per job.
- Demand base is small in absolute terms: AI referrals about 0.28% of traffic; classic local results still dominated by the map pack. The honest pitch is "fix what blocks you everywhere", not "AI is your main channel".
